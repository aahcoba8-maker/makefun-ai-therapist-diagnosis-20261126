One-line entry for the [Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41) bounty on make.fun: the funniest single-line diagnosis an AI therapist hands its human, 200 characters or less.

## The line

> Diagnosis: chronic passive-aggression — you wrote 'per my last email' three times today, and not one of them was your last email.

129 characters. Clinical voice, one specific habit, and the punchline turns on the phrase itself: none of the "last emails" was ever the last one.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the line and writes it to `entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set, as the grader does). Standard library only, no network.
